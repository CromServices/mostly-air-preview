About Andrew

One call, from the quote through to commissioning.

I began work in this trade in 1994 and now work across air conditioning, from domestic through to industrial and commercial. I have installed, repaired and maintained all types and makes.

I have lived in the Adelaide Hills all my life, so I have a complete understanding of the local climate and its heating and cooling requirements.

I am a qualified A-Class electrician, so installations do not need an extra tradesman. I use and recommend Daikin, and I will work with all types and makes.
